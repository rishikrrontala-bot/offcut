# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-selected Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind; Canvas/SVG; OpenCV.js; pretrained segmentation; pure inventory/allocation; Vitest and Playwright. No database or accounts.

## Users

Students, art clubs and teachers turning leftover coloured paper into small art.

## Product Purpose

A photographed finite inventory becomes an achievable collage and physically usable cutting/assembly plans.

## Positioning

Every output tile is one unique cuttable region of the actual photograph, with source tracing; no invented material, recolouring or duplicate reuse.

## Operating Context

4–8 separated flat opaque scraps on contrasting A4 with corners visible. Corner confirmation and mask review precede cutting. Full grids use approximately12mm squares; background cells consume material.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Implement a complete photo -> reviewed learned masks -> calibrated inventory -> capacity allocation -> texture collage -> actual-size plans. ## Product Principles

Physical correspondence is invariant. Review errors before cutting. Show shortages honestly. Preserve work on provider errors. Keep proof and submission time protected.
