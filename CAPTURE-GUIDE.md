# Real-paper proof — capture and assembly

The software is ready for real inputs. These human-created materials are still missing; software-generated drawings cannot replace them.

## First photograph

Place 4–8 flat, opaque coloured paper scraps on a contrasting **portrait A4 (210×297 mm)** sheet, with the short 210 mm edge at the top. Leave clear space between pieces and keep every sheet corner visible. Use even daylight, focus on the whole sheet, and avoid cast shadows. Larger scraps provide enough unique 12 mm squares; a 6×6 artwork needs 36 squares including its background. Photograph with your phone during the event. Retain the original and capture time.

Upload the photo in OFFCUT, confirm sheet corners, review the learned masks and choose a simple target image you own or may use. Send the original camera photo and exported OFFCUT project into this chat for inspection; do not paste API keys.

## Ten-photo evaluation

Capture ten new images, varying angle, shape, lighting, mild shadow and colour scarcity within supported conditions. Record each capture time and what changed. Keep the original files. We will record model time, usable squares, correction effort, allocation/export and exclusion behavior for each, including failures.

## Physical proof

Download one 6×6 print plan. Open the HTML file and print actual size 100%, with fit-to-page disabled. Measure both sides of the 20 mm square with a ruler and photograph that measurement. Do not cut until scale is correct. Use source-page coordinates and numbered Source IDs; overlapping pages can repeat a number, which represents the same piece and must be cut only once.

Cut the numbered 12 mm squares and arrange them using the assembly plan. Use paper glue on a backing sheet if desired. Photograph original scraps, printed plans, software preview and the final physical collage together. Record any mismatched cuts or appearance; keep the result honest.

For the final video, record a clear short clip of the actual scraps and assembly beside the app. The public 2–3-minute demo will show one real workflow, source tracing, paper exclusion and this physical result.
